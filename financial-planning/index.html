Tailored Financial Plans

Your financial life is unique. Your plan should be too. Cornerstone builds individualized strategies that serve your interests – not product commission structure.

The Industry vs. Our Approach

Most firms use standardized questionnaires and pre-built portfolios. We start from scratch with focus on you.

The Typical Firm

  • Investors are sorted into four broad categories “Buckets”; Conservative, Balanced, Growth or Income
  • These labels lead to pre-made portfolios that prioritize firm risk management over cleint performance
  • Advisors often receive commission for placing assets in specific financial instruments
  • Commission structures can create conflicts of interest between what pays the advisor and what benefits you
  • Limited flexibility to respond to your evolving life circumstances and goals

Our Cornerstone Approach

  • Individualized plans built around the unique needs, goals and risk profile for each of our clients
  • No pre-made portfolios – every strategy is constructed from the ground up for you
  • Detached from product commission structure, so recommendations are always in your interest
  • Client can be confident their assets are invested in instruments that wholly benefit them
  • Continuous review and adjustment as your goals and market conditions evolve
